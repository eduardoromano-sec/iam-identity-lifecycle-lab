# Segregation of Duties Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to reduce the risk of fraud, unauthorized access, and misuse of privileged permissions by preventing incompatible responsibilities from being assigned to the same identity.

## Principles

NovaTech Solutions applies Segregation of Duties (SoD) to sensitive business and administrative processes.

No individual should have end-to-end control over a critical process.

## Finance

Financial responsibilities must be separated between payment creation and payment approval.

### Financial Analyst

Allowed:
- Create payment requests
- View financial information required for assigned activities

Not allowed:
- Approve their own payment requests

### Finance Manager

Allowed:
- Review payment requests
- Approve authorized payments

## Privileged Access

Users must not approve their own requests for privileged access.

Privileged access requests require approval from an authorized manager or security representative.

## Access Reviews

Users must not be solely responsible for certifying their own access.

Managers or designated access owners must review sensitive permissions.

## Conflicting Access

Examples of conflicting permissions include:

| Permission A | Permission B | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| Create Payment | Approve Payment | Fraud or unauthorized payment |
| Request Privileged Access | Approve Privileged Access | Unauthorized privilege escalation |
| Assign Administrative Role | Approve Administrative Role | Excessive administrative control |
| Use Privileged Access | Certify Own Access | Ineffective access review |

## Exceptions

Exceptions must:

1. Have a documented business justification
2. Be approved by an authorized owner
3. Have a defined expiration date
4. Be periodically reviewed
5. Include compensating controls when necessary
