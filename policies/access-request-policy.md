# Access Request Policy

## Purpose

The purpose of this Access Request Policy is to ensure that users have only the access necessary to perform their daily responsibilities.

Access beyond the permissions assigned by default must be requested through a formal ticket and approved by the appropriate manager and/or application owner before being granted.

## Scope

This policy applies to all users who require access to NovaTech Solutions' corporate systems, applications, and information resources.

This includes:

- **Employees:** who require access beyond the permissions assigned by default based on their role or department.
- **Contractors and third parties:** who require access to company resources and must be subject to additional validation, defined access duration, and appropriate sponsorship.
- **Corporate systems and applications:** including internal, cloud-based, and other business systems that require controlled access to company resources.

Access to sensitive or privileged resources may require additional approval and security controls based on the level of risk involved.

## Access Principles

All access requests must follow the principles below:

### 1. Business Need

Access must only be granted when there is a valid and documented business need. Requests for access beyond the user's standard role must include an appropriate business justification.

### 2. Least Privilege

Users must receive only the minimum level of access required to perform the requested activity. If the requested permission exceeds the actual business requirement, a more appropriate level of access must be considered.

### 3. Time-Bound Access

Temporary access must have a defined expiration date whenever possible. Extensions must require renewed validation, justification, and approval before the existing access expires.

### 4. Risk and Resource Validation

Access to sensitive, privileged, or restricted resources may require additional approval from the appropriate Application Owner or Security team based on the associated risk.

Requests involving licensed or limited resources may also require validation of resource availability and business necessity.

### 5. Auditability and Traceability

Access requests, approvals, modifications, provisioning, extensions, and revocations must be documented to maintain an appropriate audit trail.
